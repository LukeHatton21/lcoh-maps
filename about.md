## About

The GreenHydrogenNinja allows you to estimate the levelised cost of green hydrogen produced from hybridised solar and wind at a high spatial granularity. It aims to provide a global, transparent and user-friendly framework for examining the economics of green hydrogen production under different cost and technology assumptions

## Contact
The GreenHydrogen.Ninja was developed by a team of researchers at Imperial College London, with the model and user interface developed by Luke Hatton (PhD researcher). He is contactable by email at l.hatton23@imperial.ac.uk
